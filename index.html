Effect settings

Noise gate silences everything quieter than that level, measured from your loudest moment. Raise it (for example -15) to cut more fan noise, lower it (for example -30) if it cuts your words. Pick an effect above to load its settings, then fine-tune here. Changes apply to the same recording. Reverb amount is the one to adjust if the echo sounds too weak or too strong.
